Veil Ainsley, once only identifiable to the name Ashcroft. This man was once only a gray-zoned hitman prior to joining the League of Villains after years of his stubborn stance of being a Vigilante. Commonly identified by his consistent stoic demeanor, long white coat, and boldened ringed eyes; the first to reappear after vanishing.

In one of more rare cases between quirks, his ability — initially classified as Emberlucence — was partially formed in his early teens. Allowing him to vanish upon the presence of heat, or push it to others by contact to the point of "ash" which he himself can manipulate and create.

Over time, his meta-abilities evolved due to his lifestyle with the Ripper and his alliance to hero's before and throughout the era of the Paranormal Liberation Army.

Appearance

Standing a large 6'2" in Japan doesn't always come easy to his approach. Oftenly bumping his head in more rural areas of the country before becoming more coordinated. In several arcs, he houses himself in a dark gray compression top, white trench coat, and techware pants.

This eventually swaps out for his dark coat in epilogue to make up for his massive wingspan of 25ft (or 12'5" per wing).

Personality

A stubborn man, and just as much of a chef. Many of his years were left to his intelligence alone rather than a quirk. For several years prior, while he was a vigilante (19 years and below), his slim build was a result of his practice in calisthenics and dysmorphia. Depending on who you are, he can be humorous and carefree to start. Yet on the job, he could be quite intimidating and stoic. When better suited to his personal pride and braced wings, he can hold the line.

With a natural independence from the start, it is no surprise he can be a natural leader and a risk-taker. Formal to a point, his teen years were primarily solitary before Shinomori. Bold, but never out in the open. Cocky, but mindful of how he hunted as a hitman in cleaning up messes. Strong-willed, but limited around other people until his (now lover) best friend and the League of Villains shoved him into his full power.

Given the fact his quirk was rather weak throughout the years, being a mix of whole-body strength and deception is why he can appear a bigger threat than he is — or even was before properly blossoming Irisphoenica violiris. If anything, he is a rage-baiting, close-range fighter, as he didn't sustain the rage behind a fire quirk. All he needed was a singular touch, and he made use of that in any way possible to misdirect his targets for a massive disadvantage.

However, beyond the point of his stubborn and distant personality, known for vanishing quickly after helping or handling a job, there are situations like what happened to Shinomori: even as a hitman, he refused to kill off the salvageable and sane. While The Ripper was known for his killings, drinking, and assaults as far as the dystopian public saw, sanity glimmered in labeled psychopaths. Remorse existed; choices weren't mentally made by him — but by his quirk. When introduced to the mixed body language in the actress, Veil only needed so long to see what was secondhand. While it was true that his body rejected everything but cannibalization (later found out because he wasn't human), some scenes still sickened the giant in each mental break. The mercy took a risk to domesticate his lover, and as many claw marks as it left behind, the killings lowered and redirected; a 'cannibal' and a hitman, targeting the foulest of mankind. In return, Shinomori reinvested in Veil's self-love, rather than being 'Ashcroft'. Properly distributing his calorie intake to eat more often with the things that tasted like ash in his own mouth, making him into the powerful leading figure Veil is known as, even if he struggles at times in the epilogue.

Abilities

Irisphoenica violiris

Emberlucence (Previously)

Being the most notable ability of his quirk, along with what gave it its original name in the file, his natural generation of silver across his skin allows him to completely vanish with subtle distortion. However, with the introduction of his violet flames, he can push his exhaustion for the reward of being completely invisible to the naked eye.

The first thing ever recognized about his abilities, the term Emberlucence, branches into several aspects of what was once only the start of his abilities. Most keenly known for invisibility, yet it is far more than just any fire quirk due to its unique nature. While at the time it was marked as a C+, the more he was observed, the more accurately it was measured as a B in its era. Each section further describes the evolutionary progression of his power, further evolving with every awakening out of his lifestyle. Spotting his invisibility is difficult for many to accomplish, yet, not impossible in its usual status. If he is to generate his bare heat, Ashcroft can only hold his distortion for 10 minutes at a time, noticeable by the bend of light itself.

In accordance with our reports, it has been established that Ashcroft later developed fire of his own around the same time he began associating with the League of Villains, roughly recorded when Endeavor was found unconscious by Hawks. This was originally covered up to be 'a gas leak' on one of the skyscrapers the pro-hero flew into. To what we can comprehend about this extensive range of fire. Ashcroft, with his record-breaking degrees of heat, had grown to rival the blue flame user, Dabi, in terms of sheer power with his explosive yet fluid-like variant of fire. However, in comparison to just those variables alone and not measuring combat intelligence, Dabi still remained one of the most potent flame quirk users of his time due to his dedication and overruling experience all at once. Whereas Ashcroft's closest ties of 'volume' are dependent on just sweat alone, similar to the hero "Great Explosion Murder God Dynamight," which takes time to build up to actually do devastating collateral damage.

Beautiful but deadly, comes as a fitting phrase for what seems to be the state of this powerful quirk's conclusion. While he remains just as flammable as anyone else internally, it turns out the majority of silver in his body belonged to his unformed wings. The first sighting of these massive wings was by Hawks at the gathering of the Paranormal Liberation Front's initial meeting, 3 months before the hero's ambush on their headquarters: the Gunga Mountain Villa. These feathers well known for the way they cast hidden colors in direct sunlight, these prismatic feathers showcase an elegant ombré transition from dioxazine purple to greige at their tips, where, just like his hair, they remain softly fluffy the more often he vanishes.

  • Physical Recovery: Outside of his calisthenics, his silver allows him to focus on recovery, sometimes more focused healing if he wants to sacrifice his invisibility and feel the same pain 2x worth.
  • Invisibility: Given his silver-embodiment, all it takes is a bit of heat to bend like a mirror. Whilst heat thats only so much leads to a "patchy" state.
  • Embers: Relating to that of his quirks first signs. He can create, manipulate, and control ember 'flakes' on their own. Later used for entities like his fake tail.
  • Violet Flames: Sweat-based flame generation, with his sweat acting as an oil fire with explosive potential.

His invisibility exposes itself significantly when exposed to rain or moisture, as his heat distortion and/or steam becomes visually traceable. Whilst his feathers were later proven to work as a physical aid similar to his personal ability to recover. Where the more feathers are together on one persona, the stronger their influence can be to recover, or simply soothe a person by being kept warm.
